[h1]Company at the table[/h1]

When we shared [b]the road ahead[/b] for [b]Farkle: Tavern Dice[/b], one plan got more questions than any other: [b]multiplayer[/b]. We're happy to confirm it's coming in the [b]next update[/b].

Dice have always been better with company. Soon you won't just play against the tavern's regulars – you'll be able to sit down with friends, rivals and fellow travellers from all over the world.

[img]multiplayer_announcement.png[/img]

[hr][/hr]

[h2]What's coming[/h2]

[img]multiplayer_features.png[/img]

[h3]🎲 Quick Match[/h3]
Looking for a game? Hit Quick Match and we'll seat you with another player who's looking for one too.

[h3]🍺 Open your table[/h3]
Don't want to wait? Start a game against the AI and [b]make your table public[/b]. You play right away, and whenever another player comes looking for a match, they can [b]take the AI's seat[/b] mid-game – no lobby, no waiting around. If a player leaves, the AI takes their seat back and the game goes on.

[h3]🤝 Invite your Steam friends[/h3]
Invite friends straight from your [b]Steam friends list[/b], or join a friend's table from the Steam overlay.

[h3]💬 Voice and text chat[/h3]
Talk across the table with [b]voice chat[/b], or keep it to [b]text chat[/b]. With players you don't know, voice starts switched off, and you can mute anyone at any time.

[h3]⏳ Fair play[/h3]
Every online roll is decided fairly for both players – nobody can load the dice. A turn timer keeps games moving, so nobody holds the table hostage.

[hr][/hr]

[h2]Further down the road[/h2]

Multiplayer is the first step of "Company at the table". After it, we're planning:

[list]
[*][b]Ranked matches and leaderboards[/b]
[*][b]A progression system[/b] – win matches to earn in-game coins and unlock [b]special dice[/b] with their own quirks
[*][b]Cosmetics[/b] – the only thing that will ever cost money. Special dice are earned by playing, never sold.
[/list]

[i]No release date has been set yet. Features and scope may change as development continues.[/i]
