[h2]Roll. Risk it. Bank it.[/h2]

Farkle is the classic push-your-luck dice game. Every turn begins with six dice:

[list]
[*]Roll and look for scoring combinations
[*]Keep your scoring dice and reroll the rest
[*]Bank your points and play it safe – or roll again for more
[*]Roll nothing that scores and you [b]Farkle[/b], losing everything from that turn
[/list]

First to [b]3,000 points[/b] wins. The rules are simple. The decisions are not.

[h2]A real tavern, not just a table[/h2]

Every match takes place inside a detailed [b]3D medieval tavern[/b] – warm firelight, wooden beams, hanging garlic, physical dice and the crackle of the hearth. The soundtrack features [b]authentic medieval music from the period itself[/b], so the tavern sounds the way it might have centuries ago.

[h2]Made in a homemade engine[/h2]

Farkle: Tavern Dice is built entirely in [b]Ptero[/b], my own custom game engine – from the renderer and lighting to the audio and UI. Seeing it ship as a real game on Steam means a lot, and I hope you enjoy playing it as much as I enjoyed building it.
