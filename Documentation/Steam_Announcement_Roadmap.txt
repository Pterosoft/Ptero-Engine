The Road Ahead: What's Next for Farkle: Tavern Dice

BODY (Steam BBCode — paste into the event description editor)
------------------------------------------------------------------

Greetings, travellers!

Thank you to everyone who has pulled up a chair at our table since launch. Seeing you push your luck (and occasionally Farkle away a whole turn's worth of points) has been a joy.

Farkle: Tavern Dice is just the first night at the tavern. Several updates are already in the making, and we want to share where the game is headed.

[i]Please note: no release dates have been set for these updates. Features, scope and the order of updates may change as development continues.[/i]

[h2]01 — A New Engine Foundation[/h2]
Farkle is built on our own custom Ptero-Engine, and the next big step is moving the game to an updated engine version. This update is planned to bring:
[list]
[*][b]Better optimization[/b] across the board
[*][b]Virtualized geometry[/b] and [b]virtual shadow maps[/b] for sharper, more detailed scenes
[*][b]Intel XeSS[/b], alongside the existing DLSS and FSR options
[*][b]Voxel global illumination[/b] — letting the game run on devices that [b]do not support DXR ray tracing[/b]
[*][b]Path tracing[/b] for those who want the tavern at its most beautiful
[*][b]Ptero AA & Ptero SR[/b] — our own analytic edge anti-aliasing and spatial reconstruction
[*][b]Steam Achievements[/b]
[/list]

[h2]02 — A Tavern in More Languages[/h2]
We want everyone to feel at home at the table. Localization is planned for:
[list]
[*]German, Polish, French, Italian, Spanish, Portuguese
[*]Czech, Ukrainian, Turkish, Arabic
[*]Korean, Chinese, Japanese
[/list]

[h2]03 — Company at the Table[/h2]
Dice are always better with friends (and rivals). We're planning [b]leaderboards[/b] and [b]multiplayer[/b], bringing more ways to compete and share a game of dice.

[h2]04 — Something to Work Toward[/h2]
A [b]progression system[/b] and an [b]in-game store[/b], giving you goals to chase across many nights at the tavern.

[h2]05 — More Music by the Fire[/h2]
[b]Music extensions[/b] to expand the tavern's soundtrack, building on the authentic medieval music already playing by the hearth.
