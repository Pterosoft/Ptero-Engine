--- Short V3 — Ptero Reconstruction ---
Title:
How our upscaler keeps edges sharp 📐 #shorts
Description:
Most upscalers only see colours, so a low-resolution edge turns into a smear and thin details break up. Ptero-SR, the upscaler we built into our Ptero-Engine, keeps the exact edges of every triangle, so each pixel on an edge gets its true coverage: clean, stable edges with no ghosting. Ptero-AA uses the same edges for anti-aliasing at native resolution.

Both are in Farkle: Tavern Dice now. Find them under Settings → Upscaler / Anti-Aliasing.
(The diagram is an illustration of the technique.)

🎲 Play on Steam: https://store.steampowered.com/app/5368450/
🌐 More: https://farkle.pterosoftstudio.com/
